## Finalists

### 1. Surag (recommended)
- Meaning: सुराग़ (surāg) - "clue, trace, sign" in Hindi/Urdu. The meaning IS the
  product: every alert is a clue to follow, never a verdict.
- Fit: short, one clean pronunciation (soo-RAHG), works in Latin script,
  no government affiliation, no guilt implication, Indian-rooted but travels.
- Screen: no fintech/fraud/software-product collision found. Only unrelated
  SURAG Medical GmbH (German surgical-audio device, stylized caps).

### 2. Viveka
- Meaning: Sanskrit "discernment - distinguishing true from false". The exact
  judgment the platform supports.
- Screen: partial collision - Viveka Health (health-tech, different domain).

### 3. Tahqiq
- Meaning: Urdu "investigation, verification". Precise semantic fit.
- Screen: clean in software; harder international pronunciation (final q).

### 4. Filigree
- Meaning: intricate branching goldwork - visually evokes traced money trails,
  with a gold/finance undertone. English, international, beautiful.
- Screen: partial collision - Filigree Wealth (US wealth manager).

### 5. MuleTrace (current working name)
- Descriptive but "mule" pre-judges the account holder (guilt implication) and
  reads as industry jargon rather than a beautiful name.

## Recommendation
**Surag** - with tagline options: "Follow every clue." / "Every trail, explained."
